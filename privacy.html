Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The SixStarSteve Clipper ("the app") is a private tool used only by the operator of the SixStarSteve accounts to publish short video clips to the operator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok: with the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the app gets basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos (video.publish) to that same account. It does not access other users' data, messages, followers or analytics.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the operator's own channel.
  • Twitch: public clip information (titles, view counts, links) from the Twitch API.

How the data is stored

Access tokens are kept only on the operator's own computer and are used only to publish videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Clip titles and transcripts may be processed by Anthropic's API to write video captions. No personal data about viewers is collected.

Deleting your data

The app stores no data about anyone except the operator. You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings. Questions: sixstarsteve.clips@gmail.com
